5.2 Future work and large-scale implementation

Future work focuses on improving the model and polishing the workflow of the project. This includes further training the AI under real-world conditions, such as lighting conditions, vehicle speeds and camera angles. Further refinement of training parameters and evaluation of results will also be carried out to improve detection accuracy and consistency.

A potential large-scale implementation of the system could involve installing cameras on public transport vehicles, such as buses. As buses regularly travel along established routes, this would enable longitudinal monitoring of pavement conditions and allow defect deterioration to be tracked over time.

At the end of each day, the collected footage could be automatically uploaded to a cloud-based processing system where the trained AI model would identify and classify pavement defects. Then detected defects could then be displayed on the interactive map, allowing road maintenance crews to identify areas which require further inspection or maintenance.

Future versions of this PDI system could use a large language model (LLM) with image analysis capabilities to further assess each detected pavement defect. This will be done after the AI detection model identifies a defect, then the image will be passed onto a LLM model. The LLM model will be able to estimate the approximate size of the detect and provide a severity score based on the visible details such as width, length, depth, and surface deterioration. The LLM is different to the Defect detection AI because the defect detection AI finds the defects while the LLM analyses each detected defect in more detail.
