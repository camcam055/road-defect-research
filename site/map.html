Exploring Potential Relationships

The interactive map allows users to explore potential relationships between traffic volumes, soil conditions and pavement defects.

  • High Traffic + Many Defects:

    May indicate pavement deterioration associated with repeated vehicle loading.

  • Less Favourable Soil Conditions + Many Defects:

    May suggest that underlying ground conditions warrant further investigation.

  • Low Traffic + Many Defects:

    May indicate other contributing factors, such as pavement ageing, drainage problems or construction quality.

Important: These relationships are possible explanations rather than confirmed causes. Further engineering investigation would be required to establish whether these factors contribute to pavement deterioration.
